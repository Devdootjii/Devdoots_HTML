Class Kit — poochho, suno, padhao

Yeh page class ke waqt screen pe khula rakho. Isme 30-min ka agenda + countdown timer, topic-wise ready-made sawaal (jawab chhupa hua — pehle students se poochho, phir reveal karo), student picker (spin karke chuno, participation tally bhi), aur ek quick-fire round hai. Rule simple hai: har 5 minute me ek sawaal — koi bore nahi hoga.

Sawaal Bank — poochho, phir reveal karo

Topic chuno · sawaal poochho · "Jawab dekho" se reveal karo

Yahan taiyaar sawaal hain, topic ke hisaab se. Tareeka: sawaal padh ke poochho → 3-4 second ruko → phir "Jawab dekho" dabao. Har sawaal ke neeche likha hai ki kab poochhna hai (jaise "Loop Tracer ke 3 steps ke baad") — isse class ka har hissa juda rahega. Golden rule: har 5 minute me ek sawaal. Neeche filter se topic chuno, ya "Random sawaal" dabao — surprise element bana rehta hai.

Student Picker — kaun jawab dega?

Spin karo · naam aaye · jawab milne pe ✓ dabao (fair distribution)

Yeh page ka sabse practical tool. SPIN dabao — Harsh, Aryan ya Khusi me se ek naam aayega. Sawaal usi se poochho. Jawab mil jaye toh ✓ Jawab mila dabao — us student ka count badh jayega. Neeche tally me dikhta rahega ki kis se kitni baar poocha gaya, aur jisse sabse kam poocha gaya wo gold me highlight hoga — isse koi peeche nahi chhootega. Kyun spin se? Kyunki isse koi personally target nahi hota — "sir ne mujhe hi uthaya" wali feeling nahi aati, aur maza bhi aata hai. Naam list editable hai — naye members add kar sakte ho.

Class ke aakhir me 3 minute — energy wapas aa jaati hai

Class ke end me jab dimaag thak jata hai, tab quick-fire chalao. Ek sawaal aayega, 30 second ka timer chalega — jo pehle haath uthaye (ya spin se) wahi jawab dega. Haan/nahi wale sawaal hain, isliye koi jhijhak nahi karega. 3 minute me 5-6 sawaal ho jate hain, aur revision bhi ho jata hai.

Facilitation Tips — bore hone hi nahi dena

Chhote tricks jo class ko zinda rakhte hain

3-SECOND RULE

Sawaal poochhne ke baad kam se kam 3 second chup raho. Silence uncomfortable lagta hai, par wahi sochne ka time hai. Isse pehle jawab de diya toh sawaal ka koi faayda nahi.

"AAGE KYA HOGA?" — PREDICTION

Animation chalane se pehle poochho: "agla step me kya hoga?" — phir chalao. Prediction maangna sabse strong engagement trick hai; dimaag automatically active ho jata hai.

JAWAB NA AAYE TOH?

Seedha jawab mat do. Pehle hint do ("socho, temp variable ki zaroorat kyun padi?"), phir 3 second ruko, phir kisi aur se poochho. Reveal sabse aakhir me — warna sab seekhne ki koshish chhod denge.

"PATA NAHI" KO TODNA

Jab koi "pata nahi" bole toh bolo: "guess karo, galat bhi bilkul theek hai" — 90% baar sahi guess aata hai. Aur galat jawab pe kabhi mat ruko, bolo "achha socha, isi ke aas-paas hai".

SHANT WALE KO KAISE LAO

Jo member kam bolta hai, uske liye pair-share karo: "20 second me ek dusre se baat karo, phir batao". Pehle partner se discuss karne se confidence aa jata hai, phir poore group me bolna aasan ho jata hai.

ENERGY CHECK (10 second)

Beech me kabhi poochho: "1 se 5 tak ungli dikhao — samajh aaya?" Sab 1-2 dikhayein toh wahin ruk ke dobara samjhao. Aage badhna nahi — warna backlog ban jayega.

HAR 5 MIN ME EK SAWAAL

Yeh ek niyam pakka karo. 30 min = kam se kam 6 sawaal. Isse class interactive rahegi aur tumhe pata chalega kaun samajh raha hai. Sawaal bank me ready hain — bas filter karke poochho.

END ME "AAJ KA TAKEAWAY"

Class ke last 30 second me ek line bolo: "aaj ka takeaway — swap se sorting banti hai, recursion se merge sort". Students ko ek line yaad rehti hai; 30 lines nahi. Homework bhi usi line se jodo.

Sabse bada secret: tumhe perfect padhna nahi hai — tumhe sawaal poochhne wala banna hai. Jo padhata hai wo bolta hai; jo samjhata hai wo poochhta hai. Students khud bolenge toh samajh apne aap ban jayegi — aur tumhara kaam aasaan ho jayega. 🚩
